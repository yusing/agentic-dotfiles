# Human-flavoured writing

Write for a cold reader skimming a feed: make the product, honest benefit, and next step clear
without a design diary or polished marketing voice. This voice is for casual public prose when
the user selects the skill, not formal papers, legal text, code comments, or commit messages.

## Choose the shape

Establish the venue, reader context, and supported claim from the request. Lead with a concrete
result or the smallest useful demo before architecture or methodology. Put a practical try path
before deep reliability detail, and state the claim's limits in plain language.

Adapt the structure to the venue and material rather than filling a fixed section list. One
honest claim is enough; include another example only when it teaches a different use. Preserve
the user's requested structure and voice.

## Cold-read check

The reader should understand the point from the opening, trust any number without decoding a
methodology essay, and know how to try it or find the full instructions. Remove unsupported claims,
redundant caveats, and sections that only interest someone who already cares. End with a concrete
invitation rather than generic engagement bait.

Done when the draft is ready to paste in the intended venue, or its missing facts are explicit.
